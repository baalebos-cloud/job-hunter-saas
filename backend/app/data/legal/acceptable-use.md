# Acceptable Use Policy

To keep Baalebos fair and safe for candidates and employers, you must not:

## Accounts
- Create more than one candidate account, or create accounts to get extra free scans.
- Use temporary or disposable email addresses, or someone else's identity.
- Share your account or try to access another person's account.

## Content
- Upload a resume that is not yours, or false information you intend to present as true.
- Post fake, misleading or discriminatory job listings, or ask applicants unlawful questions (for example about religion, ethnicity, pregnancy or disability) unless the law allows it for that role.
- Upload malware, or content that is illegal, abusive or infringes someone else's rights.

## The service
- Scrape, copy or resell Baalebos data or job listings, or use automated tools against the site, except through our published API within its limits.
- Try to bypass rate limits, plan limits, security controls or two-step verification.
- Probe for vulnerabilities without permission. If you find one, report it to support@baalebo.xyz.

## Employers
- Use applicant information only to assess candidates for the role they applied to, and delete it when you no longer need it.

## What happens if these rules are broken
We may remove content, limit features, or suspend or close accounts, and report illegal activity to the authorities. If you think we made a mistake, write to support@baalebo.xyz.
