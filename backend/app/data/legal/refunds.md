# Refund Policy

This policy applies to paid Baalebos plans (Pro and Enterprise), billed monthly through Stripe.

## Cancelling
You can cancel at any time from Plans > Manage billing. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

## Refunds
- **First payment:** if you are not satisfied, ask for a refund within [7] days of your first payment on a plan and we will refund it in full.
- **Renewals:** monthly renewals are not refunded once the new period has started, except where required by law or where a fault on our side stopped you from using the service.
- **Service faults:** if Baalebos was unavailable or did not work for a significant part of a period you paid for, tell us and we will refund or credit that period fairly.

## How to ask
Write to support@baalebo.xyz from the email address on your account, with the date of the payment. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, usually within [5 to 10] business days.

## Your legal rights
Nothing in this policy affects your rights under consumer protection law.
