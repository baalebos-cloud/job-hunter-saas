# Cookie Policy

Baalebos uses very little browser storage, and none of it is for advertising or tracking you across other websites.

## What we use
| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sign-in refresh cookie | Cookie, strictly necessary | Keeps you signed in securely. Cannot be read by page scripts. | Until you sign out or it expires |
| token | Browser storage, strictly necessary | Your current sign-in session. | Until you sign out |
| bb_device_id | Browser storage, strictly necessary | A random identifier sent only when creating an account, to prevent duplicate free accounts. | Until you clear site data |
| ref_code, first_touch | Browser storage, functional | Remembers the referral link or campaign that brought you, so it is credited when you sign up. | Until you sign up or clear site data |
| lastTaskId, brandedDownloads | Browser storage, functional | Remembers your last tailored resume and a download preference. | Until you clear site data |

## No advertising or analytics cookies
We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

## Managing storage
You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you remove the strictly necessary items you will be signed out.
