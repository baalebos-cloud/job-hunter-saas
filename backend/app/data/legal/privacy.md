# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Baalebos collects, why, who it is shared with, and your rights. The data controller is [Company legal name], [registered address]. Contact: support@baalebo.xyz.

## 1. What we collect
- **Account details:** name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), country, career tracks, and optional phone number, city and LinkedIn link.
- **Resumes and tailored resumes:** the files you upload, the text read from them, and the resumes we produce for you.
- **Applications:** the jobs you track or apply to, and the details and answers you send to employers on Baalebos.
- **Security information:** sign-in times, IP address and approximate location, browser type, and a random identifier for your browser used at sign-up to prevent duplicate free accounts (we store only a keyed hash of it).
- **Payments:** handled by Stripe. We receive your plan and payment status; we never see or store your card number.
- **Support requests:** what you write to us and any screenshot you attach.

## 2. Why we use it
- To provide the service you asked for: tailoring resumes, scoring matches, job alerts, applications (performance of a contract).
- To keep accounts and the platform secure and prevent abuse, including duplicate free accounts (legitimate interest).
- To send service emails such as verification, password reset and job alerts you switched on. You can turn job alerts off at any time.
- To meet legal obligations.
We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Artificial intelligence
To tailor a resume, its text and the job description are sent to an AI model provider (currently Groq, OpenRouter or OpenAI) under their API terms. We send only what is needed for that request.

## 4. Who we share data with
- **Employers:** only when you apply to their job on Baalebos, and only what you chose to send.
- **Service providers** that run Baalebos for us: hosting (Railway, Vercel), database (Neon), email delivery (Resend), payments (Stripe), and the AI providers above. They process data only on our instructions.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.
Some providers are outside Nigeria. Where data leaves Nigeria we rely on safeguards recognised under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## 5. How long we keep it
We keep your account data while your account is open. Tailored resumes are kept according to your plan's history limit. Security logs are kept for 90 days. When you delete your account we erase your data, except what the law requires us to keep.

## 6. Your rights
You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to some processing, and withdraw consent. Export and deletion are available in Settings; for anything else write to support@baalebo.xyz. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## 7. Security
Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes, two-step verification is available (and required for staff), and access to personal data by staff is limited by role and recorded in an audit log.

## 8. Changes
We will post changes here and tell you by email if they are significant.
